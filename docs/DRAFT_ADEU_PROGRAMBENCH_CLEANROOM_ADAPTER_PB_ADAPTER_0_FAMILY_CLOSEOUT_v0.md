## Alignment Judgment

`PB-ADAPTER-0-A` created the cleanroom intake membrane: task-visible material,
stable task artifact identity, source visibility, worker access policy, and
non-authority guardrails. It made forbidden and hidden evidence unreachable
during inference rather than merely labeled, kept derived summaries from
laundering forbidden or hidden material, and did not grant probe, command,
runner, evaluator, official ProgramBench, benchmark truth, score, model
ranking, generated submission, or future-family authority.

`PB-ADAPTER-0-B` added local/reference probe planning and observation rows
under the released A visibility/access law. It treated probes as controlled
evidence creation, constrained command rows to argv shape unless shell wrapping
is explicitly reasoned, recorded stdout/stderr hashes and bounded excerpts,
captured exit/duration/timeout and filesystem-diff posture, kept local probes
non-hidden-test-equivalent, and rejected hidden evaluator output, benchmark
truth, official probe authority, out-of-scope side effects, and observation
coverage gaps.

`PB-ADAPTER-0-C` assembled released A/B rows into one reconstruction case
packet and readiness/handoff posture. It required a single adapter candidate
and task instance lineage across intake, artifact manifest, visibility,
access, guardrail, probe plan, observation log, I/O artifact index, and
filesystem side-effect rows. It made readiness fail closed for contamination,
forbidden-source exposure, hidden-evidence exposure, derived-summary exposure,
access-contract violations, probe-scope violations, hidden-test boundary
violations, sparse contamination rows, and wrong coverage kinds. It kept local
probe evidence non-authoritative, kept handoffs pressure-only, and closed only
`PB-ADAPTER-0`.

The three slices align:

- task-visible material is indexed by stable artifact identity and snapshot
  posture before worker access;
- known-visible, known-hidden, known-forbidden, support-only,
  unknown-not-indexed, and declared-absent visibility states remain distinct;
- forbidden and hidden material cannot be mounted, queried, registered as
  worker-visible, summarized for inference, or exposed through derived worker
  summaries;
- worker access contracts do not grant command or probe authority in Slice A;
- probe plans in Slice B are controlled observation plans, not open command
  authority;
- probe observations store normalized, bounded evidence rather than unbounded
  streams;
- I/O artifact and filesystem side-effect coverage is exact and typed;
- local probes remain local reconstruction evidence, not hidden-test
  equivalence, benchmark truth, official evaluator authority, benchmark score,
  or model ranking;
- case packets require released A/B refs and preserve adapter candidate and
  task instance lineage;
- readiness summaries explicitly represent contamination and fail closed when
  contamination or authority gaps are present;
- handoffs remain later-review pressure only;
- family closeout alignment closes `PB-ADAPTER-0-A/B/C` in order and does not
  select a future family;
- reconstruction execution, generated code, official ProgramBench
  participation, official runner/evaluator integration, hidden tests, source
  lookup, benchmark submission, benchmark scoring, model ranking, generated
  official submissions, arbitrary command execution, tool invocation, runtime
  transition, product authority, graph-memory authority, recursive-policy
  authority, and future-family selection remain unselected.

## Final Family Decision

- `PB-ADAPTER-0` is closed on `main` as a ProgramBench cleanroom adapter
  membrane family.
- The family provides a bounded empirical bridge:
  ProgramBench-style task-visible material can be indexed under cleanroom
  visibility/access law, local/reference probes can be represented as
  normalized observations, and released evidence refs can be assembled into a
  readiness-reviewed reconstruction case packet without claiming benchmark
  truth or execution authority.
- The next planning pressure may consider cleanroom reconstruction execution,
  larger local fixture matrices, official ProgramBench participation
  governance, benchmark-result governance, broader conceptual broker work,
  natural task to program-profile inference, multi-language realization
  overlays, V86/V87/V88 continuation, implementation-lock review, product
  review, graph-memory review, release authority, recursive-policy work, or
  another family, but this closeout does not select or authorize any of those
  families.
- Future selectors should consume the `PB-ADAPTER-0` surfaces as cleanroom
  adapter substrate and preserve their authority boundary: `PB-ADAPTER-0` can
  record cleanroom task intake, artifact identity, visibility, access,
  guardrails, local/reference probe plans, normalized probe observations, I/O
  artifact indexes, filesystem side-effect observations, case packets,
  readiness summaries, handoff pressure, and family closeout alignment; it
  does not execute reconstruction, infer from hidden tests, access original
  sources, generate official submissions, score benchmarks, rank models,
  execute arbitrary commands, invoke tools, mutate targets, productize,
  release, establish graph-memory authority, amend recursive policy
  automatically, or select the next family.
