## Alignment Judgment

`V78-A` opened source-bound runtime execution authority requests, authority
source indexes, and non-action guardrails over released `V77-C` handoff
substrate without granting authority. `V78-B` added authority decisions,
tool-use permission envelopes, command-scope authorization boundaries, and
exception registers without executing commands, invoking tools, authorizing
target mutation, or resolving blockers by prose. `V78-C` added readiness
summaries, pre-execution-authority-review handoffs, and family closeout
alignment without granting execution authority, selecting `V79`, or performing
any target family.

The three slices align:

- runtime execution authority review remains separate from command execution;
- tool-use permission envelopes remain separate from actual tool invocation;
- support or dogfood sources cannot be the only eligibility source;
- authority requirements stay typed and source-bound;
- command preflight, target refs, local command output, and passing tool
  results remain non-authorizing;
- command-scope authorization boundaries reject globs as concrete targets and
  do not authorize target mutation inside `V78`;
- grant-like decision posture remains later-review-only and must cite concrete
  authority sources;
- product and external pressure stay blocked or future-family-routed unless
  matching authority exists;
- exception rows cannot resolve blockers by prose;
- readiness summaries reference known `V78-A` and `V78-B` rows;
- warning-ready summaries cannot carry hidden blocking exceptions;
- non-product blockers remain blocked by authority, scope, telemetry, or
  rollback posture rather than being smoothed into warnings;
- handoff derivation fails closed when summary decision refs are absent;
- pre-execution-authority-review handoff means after authority review, not
  after execution, tool invocation, product authorization, external activation,
  release, or hidden dispatch;
- closeout provenance must match handoff provenance;
- family closeout alignment closes `V78` as runtime execution authority review
  only;
- command execution, tool invocation, worker assignment, dispatch execution,
  product authorization, external branch activation, experiment design, graph
  memory, living-memory authority, release authority, and recursive policy
  amendment remain unselected future territory.

## Final Family Decision

- `V78` is closed on `main` as a runtime execution authority review and
