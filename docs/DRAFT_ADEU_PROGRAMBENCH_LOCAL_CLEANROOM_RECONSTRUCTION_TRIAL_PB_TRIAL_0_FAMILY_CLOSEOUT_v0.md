and official runner/evaluator access, blocked snapshots without passed
forbidden-content screening, and did not emit outcome audit, observation
summary, remand decision, retry authority, or family closeout.

`PB-TRIAL-0-C` audited and closed the one local trial:

- single-trial observation summary;
- local-only remand decision;
- family closeout alignment.

Review hardening in `vNext+256` closed the important lineage edge: C closeout
validation now revalidates the released B execution bundle before C-specific
acceptance and closeout checks. Local acceptance requires no blockers, passed
runbook and sandbox satisfaction, execution-capture evidence,

summaries reject comparative model/retry/benchmark language. Remand decisions
cite local evidence only and cannot become retry authority. Family closeout
alignment closes only `PB-TRIAL-0-A/B/C`.
